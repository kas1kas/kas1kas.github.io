Multimedia

This abstract painting was inspired by a combination of the work from Paula Evers, a well known and respected painter in the Netherlands,(and also a former student of ours at work) and from the influence of our dedication to the development of multimedia at our language institute. From Ms. Evers' work came the inspiration for the large blue rectangular form. The other shapes and musical, computer like forms, came from my involvement at work with multimedia equipment. The CD's added color and movement from one canvas to the next. The painting was originally painted so that all the shapes continued from one to the other, but I often changed the position of one of the three to make the painting work as a whole instead of three separate individual pieces. One of the interesting aspects of this painting is that you can turn the individual canvases around to form the desired view that you like. It is interactive in the same way that multimedia is interactive. The viewer can take part in the painting which coordinates with the idea of a multimedia learning tool.
