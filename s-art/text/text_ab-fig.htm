I am working on abstract figurative work which involves mixed media painted on MDF boards and sealed with a polyester layer. Unfortunately you can't really appreciate the polyester top layer on the photos but it is very spectacular. It adds a 3-D effect which is very appealing to the eyes and tactically enticing. What I mean is you just have to touch it! The top layer is about 5 millimeters so in that space you have a play with the mixed media in physical depth. Some of the material sticks very close to the top layer while other parts lay close to the board. It is very interesting to see, a real added depth is offered to the viewer. One could call what I'm now working on a series because there is an obvious similarity between the work but I see them working as separate pieces as well which in my definition of a series work isn't really possible. It doesn't matter at all but I am enjoying this "creating" very much at the moment. I still see this as very figurative, this new work of mine, although in ways quite different from the more realistic work I had been doing. So that's what is going on at the moment with me and my work.
