Blue Elephants


This is a very different and unusual painting of mine, which was painted on a roughly gesso'd canvas over two other (in my opinion unsuccessful) paintings. That is why you see the dark splinters which is part of the painting underneath. These two unsuccessful paintings are what add to the success of this painting. I experimented with techniques which a lot of young Dutch artists are doing at the moment. I decided to invent a new technique which was literally blowing turpentine and a heavy glaze on top of the gesso with a blow-dryer. The effect is very dramatic, spontaneous and uncontrollable, i.e. it works or it doesn't. I personally find the composition very good, and the blue elephants emerged from the treatment. Funnily enough, painting #2 was called the birth of a seahorse.
