Autumn


Autumn is a very warm and powerful painting. What I like most about it is it's balance of painterly style and realism. Up close you see the spontaneous splashes of color and loose style but from a distance you see clearly the image of a woman with strong muscle tone (of course!) and power. I had a strong and detailed drawing before painting her and some of the pencil lines are still visible in the background but I decided to turpentine over the pencil to seal the lead and use it as a paint material. I am very pleased with this technique and I plan on experimenting further. I personally feel more confident and secure with a strong under painting although sometimes the painting pulls me in another direction and I ignore the original drawing. Autumn was painted quickly and stayed true to her drawing.
