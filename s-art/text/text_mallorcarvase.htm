Mallorca vase


I collect vases from everywhere I go. I've decided to do a series work using many of the interesting vases I've collected. I find the polyester really suits the flowers. The painting is very cheerful and uplifting. Color and shape are the most important elements in this painting. I do a technique I call "peeling" which gives the painting a sort of spontaneity that without the technique would be lacking. When I'm basically finished the piece I put, and varnish, a layer of tissue paper over the whole bouquet. I let this dry then I "peel" parts of the tissue paper off. You get a lot of beautiful surprises this way. Sometimes, of course, you peel off more then you bargained for and then I have to re-paint but that's okay!
