Escaping butterflies


As, what is becoming more normal than not, this painting is created on top of many other gessoed over paintings hence the structure and relief in areas. I used my blow dried heavy glaze technique and I think this abstract painting really lends itself to it. I also experimented with gold leaf and placed this on the painting in various stages of the painting. The gold that you see the most was added on last right before the last thin pink glaze that I went over the whole painting with. This last glaze really pulled the painting together as a whole without "mudding" the colors. The pink rectangle in the center is what I see as a window where butterflies are escaping out of. Color is obviously the dominating feature of this work and I suppose the beginning of Spring with it's light colors had an influence on me.
