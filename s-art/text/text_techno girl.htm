Tecno girl

This is an abstract figurative work which was influenced by techno music. It's almost impossible to photograph because the shinny almost glitter like parts that surround the figure and form her breasts are really reflective hologram paper! What happens is depending on the angle you look at the painting and the light situation different "parts" light up. The most beautiful situation of all is the painting works well even if there is no light and thus no "special effects". I painted over the paper with green glass paint to integrate the paper in the painting without this process the reflective paper was too strong and domineering and the painting "self " was lost. The reaction from children has been delightful! This is really an interactive painting because it changes with the viewers distance and light source. My only regret is that you can't experience it from a photograph.
