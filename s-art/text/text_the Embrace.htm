the Embrace


This is what I consider an exceptional piece of work due to the intimacy of the embrace and the brightness that comes though from the use of white paint. It is a painting on top of a painting and the gesso roughness comes through which adds a 3-dimensional character to the finished work. Although blue usually comes across as cold, due to the intimate nature of the position of the bodies, the painting is very warm. There is an interesting ironic feature of the fingers on the hands. The man's fingers resemble perhaps the woman's, and the woman's hand resembles that of a man. So one wonders, who is the female, who is the man? Or is it a heterosexual embrace at all? And is that important in the portrail of intimacy?
