Looking at you... looking at me


If ever there was a painting that gave me pain in creating I would say it was this one! Although I am pleased with the final outcome and would go through the process again to reach this end it was indeed a long and pain ridden experience. First physical pain because I dropped the painting, which by the way is not canvas but wood!, on my toes and wasn't able to walk for a few days. Mentally I had enormous stress in drawing my face and without exageration must have made the drawing and erased it 20 times! If that wasn't enough after getting a drawing I was satisfied with I had to gesso over a completed painted head 3 times because I over worked it and in the end it had no likeness of me at all! The image of Kas was very easy from the beginning and perhaps I over worked that a bit because it was so easy.
The inspiration for the painting came by accident. I was in my studio trying out new poses to paint in front of a mirrow and Kas was behind me looking down his digital camera ready to shoot a pose. I suddenly looked into the mirrow in front of me at him and knew he was looking through the camera at me and I found the image intriging, and I painted this image.
