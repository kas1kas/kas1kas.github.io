Now . . . What?

This painting continues to be painted in my more developed style beginning with the Bouwvakker series. It is unusual in 2 ways. One there is a face represented, although a critical feature is missing - the stare of the eyes, and two there is the surprise feature of the thrown blow dried glaze technique. Up till now I had only used this on my abstract paintings. I feel this "splash" of color adds a positive feeling to an otherwise contemplative painting. No doubt the situation in the world today has influenced the mood of my last 2 paintings. There is a soberness and "downess" to them but still they elude amazing energy and poignant emotions.
