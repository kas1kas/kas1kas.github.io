Barcelona

Is a painting/collage of my impressions of Barcelona. The mosaic at the bottom was made by me but many of the details in the painting are not well shown by the photograph. The most important impression did come through, I hope, the vibrancy, color and feeling of movement that Barcelona possesses. The city to me is a mixture of old culture and superb architect and the outlandish modern art work by Picasso and Miro. Always bright and daring and still contemporary today. It is a city that lives and I hope this feeling is represented in my painting.
