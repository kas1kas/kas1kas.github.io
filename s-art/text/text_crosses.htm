Crosses

The name comes from the crosses you see from the breasts, arms and legs of the model. The painting style was influenced by a recent trip to Germany where I saw Carmencita by Lovis Corinth. The color, especially on the left front leg of the model, is placed on in a similar fashion to the way the paint is placed on in Carmencita. At the moment I am fascinated by the way women's limbs are intertwined when they are sitting. The position is very relaxed and natural, there isn't any posing it's really quite ordinary but still fascinating, at least I find it so
