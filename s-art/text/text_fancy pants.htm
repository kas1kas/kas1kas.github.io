Fancy pants

This is a playful painting with it's strongest part being the design. It's very colorful and exciting due to the splashes of glitter and the very tiny silver stars. The idea was to give homage to a women's strong and very curvy backside. I don't find it at all erotic but more playful and eye catching. The prominent blue lines help guide your eye around and around the painting. Surprisingly the polyester had a stunning reaction on the glitter and makes it look like tiny stars are twinkling. You can't see this effect from the photograph but it's quite charming!
