Midnight

Perhaps the figure in Midnight is familiar to you because it is the same model as in Black Straps just a slightly different pose. Being one who often breaks my own rules I used black in this painting something I swore I would never do but well, rules are meant to be broken. The painting was done with a combination of oils and oil pastels. It was done in one evening and finished round about midnight hence the name but also I feel the painting has a "late night" feel to it!
