Blue stretch

Blue Stretch was made on board with mixed media and polyester poured on top. Now there were some problems with the procedure this time and if you look carefully you can see 2 major cracks in the polyester and a few minor cracks at the top. Yea . . this is still an experimental phase and "accidents" will happen! I think the problem was over use of the "harder" fixture that you have to add into the polyester. I thought if I added more it would just dry quicker and it did! It just became very brittle and broke easily and it shrunk out of it's frame which you obviously have to build around the board first so the polyester in it's liquid form doesn't run off the painting. I would love to say I have learned from my mistakes but unfortunately I did the same dumb thing again last night on a new painting and yes the same shrinking action occurred luckily it hasn't broken . . . yet!

I must honestly say this work does not lend itself to being photographed because you can't see the dimension of the top clear layer and that is really a pity because it truly is cool!
Anyway you can still get an idea of what I'm working on at the moment.
