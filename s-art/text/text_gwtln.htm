Girl with the long neck

This painting exploits 3 specific features. Color, expressionistic painting style and deliberate exaggeration of certain features, the neck in this case. I was encouraged to paint this way by my current mentors and it feels good! It is indeed much more challenging then my more realistic figure paintings and much more personal. This painting says a lot more about how I view the model and her attraction then a purely good rendition of the figure.
It is funny for I am sure people who don't paint won't believe how much more time and effort went into a painting like this compared to "Autumn" or "Anticipation". The deliberate exaggeration of the neck focuses the attention of the viewer on what I feel is such a beautiful pose. It's sort of like I wanted to offer the viewer and extra helping of what I found so appealing!
