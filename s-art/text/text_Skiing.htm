Skiing

This painting is done with a new technique I developed which uses blow drying turpentine washes and glazes over a heavily gessoed canvas. The inspiration for this painting came from a recent trip skiing. I hope the feeling of movement comes through in the photograph like it does in the real painting. There are hints of ski lifts and skiers in the composition but what catches the eye immediately is the skier in the middle and his/her flight in space right before he descends the mountain. As always movement, emotion and or position are the three things most important in any work of mine and in this particular painting movement is dominant.

Ps. I, personally, have no" flight in space movement" before descending any mountain, but some skiers do!
