Bouwvakker2 ("The construction worker")


This painting is the second part in the series Bouwvakker. I basically painted a close up of the hands and head of the model who was in the first painting. It is a slightly different pose but everything else is the same. I found it so interesting that without the background the impression you get from the painting, due to the position of the hands and head, is so different from the first painting. You sense an inner battle, a helplessness and possibly despair. It was painted in a painterly fashion and took, for me, quite a while.
