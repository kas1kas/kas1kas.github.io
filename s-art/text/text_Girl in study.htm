Girl in study


This is an unusual painting of mine because the girl is painted in an interior, something I usually don't do. Because of the bright and happy colors, there is a freshness and un complicatedness that surrounds the painting. The woman is at peace in her environment and is one with her surroundings. A peak at a spontaneous moment of a woman in her home. There is an obvious lack of heavy paint, which makes the paining perhaps more of a study than a finished work.
