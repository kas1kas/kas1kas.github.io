Black straps

The woman comes across in the painting larger than life. The dramatic use of purple and yellow to show shadow and light are unique and add to the vibrancy in the painting. The black straps are not only on the shoes but are displayed in many areas of the body and background. This adds to the continuity of the painting. Because of the pose and the shoes in the process of being taken off, there is, obviously, an erotic sphere to the painting.
