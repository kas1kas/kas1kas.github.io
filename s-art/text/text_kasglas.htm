Kas looking through the looking glass


This is a portrait of Kas. I made a template of his profile and used this to make many profiles of him. The idea is to show the many different "sides" of Kas. The polyester did something really weird this time. It seems air got caught under the layer of polyester and the MDF board so you have this really beautiful crack pattern! The frame is self made and is reminiscent of Alice in Wonderland's mirror which I thought fit quite well in this fanciful portrait theme.
