Falling asleep


This painting is a more abstract polyester work piece compared to what I have been doing. I used a lot of the photos that I made for "Looking at you looking at me" in the piece. The idea is a glimpse of the mind just before it falls asleep. Therefore the snippets of reality photos mixed between a haze of sparkles reminiscent of falling into space i.e. dream world. The way the light shines on the "milky way" line of glitters is really appealing. I view this work as a needed "break" from the reality intense painting of "Looking at you looking at me".
