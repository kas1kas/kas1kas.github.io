Selfportrait2


Oil on canvas
This is probably the most, from start to finish, creative work I have done so far. The photograph was made of me with my pose. I created the concept and then painted the picture taking liberties and moving away from the original image. I had a very difficult time with the hair and am still not 100% pleased with the outcome although the process was certainly a creative and learning experience.
I will continue in this vein further for a while.
