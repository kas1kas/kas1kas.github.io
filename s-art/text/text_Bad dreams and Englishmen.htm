Bad dreams and Englishmen

The painting has many layers and depicts the artists frenzy while in a dream state.
There are many dog and other animal images which are the re-occurring nightmare of the artist.
Phallic symbols predominate which also relate back to the artist dreams.
The use of one color is to bring some quietness to an otherwise wildly hectic painting
The thick glaze looks lovely on the painting but is not photographed well
