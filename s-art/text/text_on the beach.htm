On the beach

On the beach is a polyestered painting with a diffference! This work is more similiar to my older style but still has the added attraction of tissue paper and polyester which adds a depth in color and in texture. I definitly want to go further in this direction because I realize now how much I missed traditional painting! The body of the model is warmly portrayed in yellow anf fushia. The background has more of the tissue and is abstract in form. Allthough it looks quite bright in the photo in reality it is even brighter!
