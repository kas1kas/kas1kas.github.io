Girl in stars


Is a bit of a spoof on the perfect ,well physically defined ,anonymous women and her search for stardom! The effect of the silver stars around the canvas and under the canvas gives the viewer a dilemma of where the painting ends, or does it end? The use of the boa at the top is to give an exaggerated idea of elegance which actually translates to tackiness in reality. Also in the boa are small white lights which are connected to a battery on the back of the painting so you can actually, turn her on! This added bit only intensifies the "Hollywood" feel to the painting.
