Renee


This is a commissioned piece of Renee from her husband. The assignment was to paint the essence of Renee who is a dear friend of mine and a very special woman. It was easy to choose colors to paint such a dynamic and forceful woman but to include the subtle sides of her proved challenging. The brush strokes needed to be strong and determined and I find that easy to do but to get the essence of her persona including her less dynamic and weaker side was more difficult. Anyone can paint a dynamic person but to include the layer underneath including loneliness and insecurity which makes Renee so "real" was the real "art". I hope I achieved this, I think I have because the painting begs for a long good look and is certainly not a portrayal of only a "super woman".
