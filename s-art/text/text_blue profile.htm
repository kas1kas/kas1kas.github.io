Blue profile

Blue Profile was made on wood with gold leaf, silver leaf, oil bars, craypas, oil, paper and wall paper paint. The image in blue represents a woman in profile. The painting is very large and warm in color. There is a sense of conversation taking place from the intensity of the woman's eyes and the out of body experience. It was done in one evening and should be loosely interpreted i.e. if you don't see the position of the woman who cares? The colors are the most important part of the painting and the interaction of all the various painting materials. I am getting more abstract at the moment but I already feel a yearning for more realistic figurative work!
