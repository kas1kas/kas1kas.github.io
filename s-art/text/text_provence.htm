Provence

This was a commissioned work for a family who has a summer house in the south of France. The design was from flowers which were wild and I hope give the impression of warmth, harmony and freedom. I used a lot of salt which gives the painting texture and depth. The colors are rich and I used my blow drying glaze technique. The painting gave me a lot of stress, I don't know why, but I hope the finished piece doesn't reflect the "fight" I had in creating it
