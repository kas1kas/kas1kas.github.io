Girl with the golden leg

What I'm working on now is to paint the figure with the background instead of concentrating on the background as a separate entity and dropping blue paint on top to form the outline of a figure. Of course with traditional painting one always paints this way, but I deliberately wanted to ,"go abstract",so I consciously avoided all figurative shapes. Now I am changing the focus. I guess I just can't get away from some form of realistic figurative work! Now, as I mentioned before, I was impatient and there was some shrinking but nothing broke so it's only cosmetic problems with the frame and nothing serious. It has a polyester top layer and that is what makes it so bright and sunny!
